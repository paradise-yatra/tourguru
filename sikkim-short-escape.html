Day-by-Day Plan

Detailed Itinerary

Once you step off at NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport in Siliguri, our team will be right there to welcome you and take care of everything so you can settle in without any stress. Rest up, freshen yourself, and take it slow for a bit. As the evening rolls in, head over to MG Marg for a relaxed walk where you can browse through local handlooms, pick up a few souvenirs, or grab some beautiful handicrafts to take back home. Wind down the night at your hotel and sleep well in a room that feels just right after a long day of travel.
Start small, it gets better as the days pass by. Waterfalls, hill-top, monastery and cable car rides, experience them all in Gangtok. Kick the morning off with a hearty breakfast at the hotel before anything else. Once you are full and ready to go, our team will take you around to soak in everything Gangtok has to offer. The day will take you through some truly wonderful spots including Enchey Monastery, Bakthang Waterfalls, Ganesh Tok, Tashi Viewpoint, Do Drul Chorten Stupa, the Directorate of Handicraft and Handloom, Flower Show, Institute of Tibetology, Banjhakri Falls and the Ropeway. It is quite a full day out, so when you finally get back to your room at night, that pillow is going to feel really well deserved.
If you are in Gangtok, a trip to Tsomgo Lake is something you simply cannot skip. The lake is so breathtaking that it feels like you have walked straight into a painting. Around 8 in the morning, our team will pick you up from the hotel and take you on the drive up to Tsomgo Lake and Baba Harbhajan Mandir. The three hour uphill drive itself is a beautiful experience you will carry with you long after the trip is over. Sitting at 12,400 feet above sea level, Tsomgo Lake holds a deep sense of calm and is considered sacred by both Hindus and Buddhists in the region. You will then make your way to Baba Harbhajan Mandir, perched at 13,200 feet, where the atmosphere feels nothing short of spiritual. When you have taken it all in, our team will bring you back to the hotel. Sit down to a warm and filling dinner, then stretch out and get a good night of sleep because the days ahead are only going to get better.
Start Day 4 with a heavy breakfast as this day awaits travelling and sightseeing umpteen. On your journey to Pelling you will come across various sightseeing destinations of religious importance in Namchi and Ravangla. Our team will come pick you up from the hotel in the morning and set off toward Pelling, a quiet and charming little hamlet tucked away in west Sikkim. Along the way, you will get to stop at Namchi Char Dham and the Samdruptse Statue, two places that sit at the very heart of Sikkim's culture and spiritual life. After spending some time there soaking it all in, the journey continues to Ravangla where Buddha Park and the Temi Tea Gardens are waiting to be explored. The views of the Greater Himalayas from Ravangla are the kind that genuinely stop you in your tracks. Once you have had your fill of all of this, you will finally roll into Pelling and check in at the hotel. A good meal and a comfortable night of rest will set you up perfectly for whatever the next day brings along.
You will get the chance to visit the best of Pelling - it's a beautiful hill station adorned with some of the most beautiful waterfalls, monastery, lakes and valleys. After breakfast, the day opens up beautifully as our team takes you around to discover what makes Pelling so special. You will visit Darap Valley, Rimbi Waterfalls, Rock Garden, Kanchenjunga Waterfalls and the sacred Khecheopalri Lake, all of which are the kind of places that remind you just how stunning the natural world can be. Take your time in each spot and let the surroundings sink in. The day also brings you to Rabdentse Ruins, Pema Yangtse Monastery and the Helipad Ground, places that carry a quiet sense of history and give you a real feel of the rich Buddhist culture that runs deep through this land. When the sightseeing wraps up, our team will bring you back to the hotel. Treat yourself to some authentic Sikkimese food for dinner, then lie back and let the memories of the day wash over you as you drift off to sleep.
Well, every story has an end. With packed bags of goodies and memories umpteen, journey back home. On the final day, check out of the hotel and make your way back to where this whole adventure first began, either NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport. The bags may be heavier with little goodies and souvenirs picked up along the way, but it is the memories tucked inside your heart that weigh the most. Every laugh, every view, every quiet moment up in those hills, hold onto all of it. And on those days when life feels a little too loud and you need a breather, just flip back through these pages and let those memories bring a warm smile back to your face. They always will.

Packing Guide

What to Carry

Hill stations & winding mountain roads require smart packing - here's what you must bring.

Warm Clothing

Thermals, woollen layers and a windproof jacket - evenings in Gangtok and Pelling can get quite chilly even in summer.

Valid ID Proof

Aadhaar, Voter ID or Passport is mandatory. An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is required for restricted zones - we arrange this for you.

Comfortable Footwear

Grip-sole walking shoes or sneakers are essential for monastery visits, viewpoints and uneven terrain around Pelling.

Basic Medicines

Motion sickness tablets for winding mountain roads, pain relievers, band-aids and any personal prescription medicines.

Day Backpack

A light 20-25L backpack for daily excursions with water bottle, snacks, camera and travel essentials.

Rainwear / Poncho

Sudden showers are common in Sikkim's hills - a lightweight poncho or rain jacket keeps you comfortable and camera-ready.

Pro Tips

Travel Tips

1

Carry your ILP documents. Inner Line Permit is required for Tsomgo Lake and certain restricted zones in Sikkim. We help arrange this - keep copies handy throughout the trip.

2

Start Tsomgo Lake early. Leave by 8 AM as recommended. The drive takes 3 hours each way and popular viewpoints get crowded by midday - early birds get the best of it.

3

Stay hydrated on mountain roads. Long drives on winding Sikkim roads can cause fatigue and motion sickness. Keep water and light snacks in the car at all times.

4

Carry cash. ATMs are scarce in Pelling and remote sightseeing areas. Withdraw enough from Gangtok before heading to west Sikkim.

5

Respect local customs. Remove footwear before entering monasteries, dress modestly and maintain silence inside places of worship - locals appreciate it deeply.

Best Time

When to Visit

March - May

Best season - mild weather, rhododendrons in bloom and crystal clear views of Kanchenjunga.

Oct - Dec

Post-monsoon - crisp air, stunning mountain views and fewer crowds at all major spots.

June - Sept

Avoid - heavy monsoon, frequent landslides and road blockages make travel difficult and unsafe.

Jan - Feb

Snow season - Tsomgo Lake freezes beautifully. Cold but magical for travellers who love snow.
