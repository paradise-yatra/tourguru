Day-by-Day Plan

Detailed Itinerary

Upon arriving in Corbett, you will be warmly received and transferred to your hotel for a relaxed check-in. Give yourself some time to settle in and soak up the natural surroundings that make this destination so special. In the evening, head out to the revered Garjia Devi Temple, perched dramatically on a rock in the middle of the Kosi River, and then explore the fascinating Corbett Museum - a wonderful introduction to the wildlife heritage and legacy of Jim Corbett himself. Head back to the hotel for a warm dinner and a peaceful overnight stay.
Rise early and kick off the morning with an optional Jeep Safari through the stunning Jim Corbett wilderness - the thrill of spotting tigers, elephants, and leopards in their natural habitat makes for an absolutely unforgettable start to the day. Head back for breakfast before checking out and hitting the road towards the charming hill town of Nainital. Once you arrive and check into your hotel, spend the evening enjoying a relaxing boat ride on the beautiful Naini Lake, a peaceful visit to the Naina Devi Temple, and a leisurely stroll along the lively Mall Road. Comfortable overnight stay in Nainital.
Start your day with an exhilarating cable car ride to Snow View Point, where the panoramic Himalayan vistas are nothing short of breathtaking. Then make your way to Tiffin Top (Dorothy's Seat) for another sweeping view of the surrounding valley. Spend the morning exploring the fascinating Nainital Zoo, the impressive Raj Bhawan, and the unique Eco Cave Garden. In the afternoon, take a short excursion to the tranquil Bhimtal Lake - a quieter and equally scenic alternative to Naini Lake, with a small island aquarium in the middle. As the evening rolls in, head back to Mall Road for shopping and some local snacks. Overnight stay in Nainital.
After an early breakfast, check out and hit the road towards Mussoorie - the Queen of Hill Stations. The drive takes you through the scenic Haridwar'Dehradun route, treating you to lovely views of the Shivalik foothills along the way. En route, you may stop at Haridwar Har Ki Pauri for a quick darshan if time permits. Once you arrive in Mussoorie, check into your hotel, settle in, and breathe in the cool and refreshing mountain air. Spend the evening strolling on the famous Mall Road. Peaceful overnight stay in Mussoorie.
Spend your day exploring some of Mussoorie's most beloved attractions. Begin with the stunning Kempty Falls - a magnificent multi-tiered waterfall surrounded by hills - followed by a visit to the beautifully maintained Company Garden. Take the ropeway up to Gun Hill, the second highest point in Mussoorie, where the sweeping views of the Himalayas and the Doon Valley are genuinely breathtaking. Make your way to Lal Tibba, the highest point in Mussoorie, for a telescope view of the snow-capped Gangotri, Yamunotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath ranges. As the evening rolls in, head to Mall Road to browse local shops and enjoy the cool mountain breeze. Comfortable overnight stay in Mussoorie.
After a leisurely breakfast, check out of the hotel and take a quiet moment to reflect on all the wonderful experiences this trip brought - tiger trails of Corbett, the shimmering lakes of Nainital, and the misty peaks of Mussoorie. Your private transfer to Dehradun airport or railway station will be arranged at your convenience. Head home carrying a heart full of cherished Himalayan memories.

Packing Guide

What to Carry

Hill stations and jungle safaris need smart packing - here's your essential checklist.

Warm & Layered Clothing

Mussoorie and Nainital evenings are cool year-round. Carry a fleece, light jacket or woollen layer for comfort.

Valid ID Proof

Aadhaar, Voter ID or Passport is mandatory for hotel check-ins and Jim Corbett safari permits.

Comfortable Walking Shoes

You'll be walking a fair bit on Mall Roads and sightseeing spots. Comfortable closed-toe shoes are a must.

Basic Medicines

Motion sickness tablets for the winding hill roads, pain relievers, band-aids and any personal prescription medicines.

Day Backpack

A light 15-20L backpack for daily excursions carrying water, snacks, sunscreen and a light jacket.

Rainwear / Poncho

Sudden rains are very common in Mussoorie and Nainital - a compact poncho or rain jacket is highly recommended.

Pro Tips

Travel Tips

1

Book the Corbett safari in advance. Jim Corbett safari slots - especially for the Dhikala zone - fill up very fast. Book at least 2-4 weeks ahead for best zone options.

2

Visit Nainital sights early. The cable car queue at Snow View Point gets long after 10 AM. Arrive by 8:30 AM to beat the crowds and get clearer Himalayan views.

3

Keep cash handy. UPI works at most larger shops, but small stalls at Kempty Falls, Bhimtal, and ropeway counters often prefer cash. Withdraw before heading out.

4

Carry motion sickness tablets. The hairpin bends on the roads to Nainital and Mussoorie can be challenging. Keep tablets ready, especially for children and elderly travellers.

5

Wear muted colours for the safari. Bright colours can scare away wildlife. Opt for khaki, olive, or brown shades during the Corbett jeep safari for better wildlife sightings.

Best Time

When to Visit

Feb - May

Best season - pleasant weather, Corbett safari fully open, clear Himalayan views from Mussoorie.

Sept - Nov

Post-monsoon - lush green hills, crystal-clear lakes, great wildlife activity in Corbett.

July - Aug

Avoid - heavy rains, landslide risk on hill roads, Corbett Dhikala zone closes during monsoon.

Dec - Jan

Cold but scenic - Mussoorie can see snowfall; great for couples and snow lovers. Carry heavy woolens.
