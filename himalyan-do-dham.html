Day-by-Day Plan

Detailed Itinerary

As soon as you step off at Haridwar Railway Station, our representative will be there to greet you and escort you to your hotel. Once you have checked in and freshened up, head out to explore some of Haridwar's most beloved spots, including the iconic Har Ki Pauri and the revered Mansa Devi Temple. As evening rolls in, make your way back to Har Ki Pauri to witness the breathtaking Ganga Aarti, a moment that is truly hard to put into words. After a fulfilling day of sightseeing, return to the hotel and settle in for a peaceful night stay.
After breakfast, check out of the hotel and hit the road towards Guptkashi or Sitapur. The drive treats you to some really beautiful scenery, with Moolgarh and Lambgaon adding a lovely touch to the journey along the way. Once you reach your destination, check into your hotel room, unwind, and give yourself some time to relax. Enjoy a restful overnight stay at Guptkashi or Sitapur.
Wake up around 4 AM and get moving towards Kedarnath without much delay. You will first hit the base point and from there, the 18 km journey to Kedarnath begins. Whether you choose to trek, ride a pony or palki, or take a helicopter (available at an extra cost), pick whatever suits your comfort and energy levels best. Once you arrive, check into your camp or hotel, freshen up, and then make your way to Kedarnath Temple for a deeply moving darshan experience. If you have some energy left, a visit to Bhairav Temple is well worth it too. Settle in for a peaceful overnight stay at Kedarnath.
After your morning darshan, lace up and begin making your way down the trek towards Guptkashi. Once you arrive, get your hotel check-in done, settle into your room, and allow yourself some well deserved rest after the long and fulfilling day. Enjoy a comfortable overnight stay at Guptkashi.
After breakfast, get on the road and drive your way towards Badrinath. Upon arrival, check into the hotel and take a little time to settle in before heading out. Make your way to Tapta Kund for a holy bath, a wonderfully refreshing experience, and then walk over to Badrinath Temple to attend darshan and soak in the deeply spiritual atmosphere. Once done, head back to the hotel and enjoy a peaceful overnight stay.
After breakfast, make your way to Mana Village and spend some quality time exploring its wonderful attractions, including Bhim Pul, Vyas Gufa, the Saraswati River, Vasudhara Waterfall, and the charming Last Tea Shop. It is a place that feels genuinely special, so take your time and enjoy every bit of it. Once you are done, hit the road and drive towards Rudraprayag or Srinagar. On arrival, settle into your hotel room and enjoy a relaxing night stay
After breakfast, it is time to bid farewell as you begin your return journey towards Haridwar Railway Station for your onward departure.

Packing Guide

What to Carry

High altitude requires smart packing - here's what you must bring.

Warm Clothing

Thermals, woollen layers, windproof jacket - temperatures drop below 0°C at Kedarnath & Badrinath.

Valid ID Proof

Aadhaar, Voter ID or Passport is mandatory for all travellers. Keep multiple copies.

Trekking Shoes

Comfortable, grip-sole shoes are essential for Yamunotri and Kedarnath treks.

Basic Medicines

Altitude sickness pills, pain relievers, band-aids and any personal prescription medicines.

Day Backpack

A light 20-25L backpack for daily excursions with water bottle, snacks and essentials.

Rainwear / Poncho

Sudden rains are common in the Himalayas - a lightweight poncho or rain jacket is a must.

Pro Tips

Travel Tips

1

Start early each day. Temples get very crowded by 9 AM. Aim to reach by 6-7 AM for a peaceful darshan experience.

2

Stay hydrated. High altitude can cause dehydration quickly. Carry at least 2 litres of water daily and avoid alcohol.

3

Book Kedarnath helicopter in advance. Helicopter seats sell out weeks before - book as soon as you confirm your package.

4

Carry cash. ATMs are scarce beyond Uttarkashi and Guptkashi. Withdraw enough before leaving Haridwar.

5

Respect local customs. Remove footwear before temples, dress modestly, and maintain silence inside shrines.

Best Time

When to Visit

May - June

Peak season - pleasant weather, temples fully open, rhododendrons in bloom.

Sept - Oct

Post-monsoon - clear skies, best mountain views, less crowded temples.

July - Aug

Avoid - heavy monsoon, frequent landslides, road closures possible.

Nov - Apr

Temples closed - Char Dham portals shut for winter after Diwali typically.
