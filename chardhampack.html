Day-by-Day Plan

Detailed Itinerary

Your journey begins with a pickup before 12 noon from the New Delhi Airport or Railway Station, where our driver will be right there to welcome you and help you get started on a smooth and comfortable ride to Haridwar. Once you arrive, check into the hotel, settle in, and give yourself some time to unwind after the travel. Later in the evening, head over to Har ki Pauri to witness the magical Ganga Aarti, an experience that stays with you long after it ends. Return to the hotel for dinner and a restful overnight stay.
After breakfast, hit the road from Haridwar and make your way towards Barkot. The drive is packed with wonderful stops along the way, including Shiv Mandir, the lively hill town of Mussoorie, and the gorgeous Kempty Falls. Do not miss Bhatta Falls and the famous Maggie Point in Mussoorie where the Maggie is honestly worth every bite, and take a leisurely stroll along Mall Road before continuing your journey. Once you reach Barkot, check into your hotel, get comfortable in your room, and settle in for a peaceful night stay.
Wake up early, have a good breakfast, and drive to Jankichatti. The Yamunotri Temple is about 6 km from there, and you can either hop on a horse or trek your way up, with most people reaching the temple in around 2 hours. Once you arrive, take a refreshing bath at Jamunabai Kund, visit the temple, and seek the blessings of Maa Yamuna during darshan. Head back to Jankichatti by horse, palki, or on foot, whichever suits you best. By evening, make your way back to your hotel in Barkot, rest up, and enjoy a warm dinner before settling in for a comfortable overnight stay.
After breakfast, check out of the hotel and hit the road towards Uttarkashi. On the way, stop by Shivgufa Temple for a quick and peaceful visit before continuing your drive. Once you arrive in Uttarkashi, head straight to the Kashi Vishwanath Temple, fondly known as Chota Kashi Temple in Uttarakhand, and spend some time soaking in its spiritual warmth. Afterwards, check into your hotel, kick back, and relax. Enjoy a satisfying dinner and a restful overnight stay at the hotel.
After breakfast, head out from Uttarkashi and make your way towards Gangotri Temple. During the drive, stop at Gangnani for a relaxing hot water bath before getting back on the road. As you continue, the stunning views of Harshil Valley and Lanka Bridge make the journey feel like a treat in itself. Once you reach Gangotri, visit the temple and take in the peaceful spiritual atmosphere. After the temple visit, begin your drive back to Uttarkashi, where a warm dinner and a comfortable overnight stay at the hotel will be waiting for you.
After breakfast, check out of the hotel and begin your drive towards Guptkashi. The route takes you past the charming spots of Moolgarh and Lambgoan, so keep your eyes open and enjoy the scenery along the way. By evening, arrive at your hotel in Guptkashi, check in comfortably, and settle in for a relaxing overnight stay.
Start your day bright and early and make your move towards Kedarnath Temple. The 18 km trek can be covered in whatever way works best for you, whether that is walking, riding a horse or palki, or taking a helicopter if you want to save time and energy (available at an extra cost). By afternoon, check into your hotel or camp in Kedarnath, freshen up, and then head to the temple for darshan of Lord Shiva, one of the 12 Jyotirlingas. Do not miss the Bhairav Temple either, sitting just above Kedarnath and absolutely worth the short climb. Once you are done, make your way back down and drive to Guptkashi for a peaceful overnight stay at the hotel.
In the morning, make your way down the trek to Gaurikund and then hit the road towards Guptkashi, where a satisfying dinner and a comfortable night stay will be ready to welcome you.
After breakfast, check out and begin your drive from Guptkashi towards Badrinath. Once you arrive, check into the hotel and settle in before heading over to Tapta Kund for a relaxing and spiritually refreshing dip in the warm waters.
After breakfast, head out to Mana Village, a place that is truly special and well known for its Last Tea Shop, Vasundara Falls, Bhim Pul, Vyas Gufa, and the serene Saraswati River. Take your time exploring each of these spots as they each carry their own unique charm. Once you are done with the sightseeing, continue your journey towards Rudraprayag and make sure to stop at Narsingh Temple in Joshimath along the way. By evening, arrive at your hotel, check in, and settle down for a relaxing overnight stay.
After breakfast, pack up and begin your drive towards Haridwar. The journey itself is full of meaningful stops, including the Dhari Devi Temple, the sacred confluence at Devprayag, and the spiritually vibrant town of Rishikesh, all of which make the drive back a memorable one. Once you reach Haridwar, check into your hotel and enjoy a peaceful overnight stay.
After a relaxed breakfast, it is time to wrap up the trip as you get transferred to the Delhi Airport or Railway Station for your journey back home.

Packing Guide

What to Carry

High altitude requires smart packing - here's what you must bring.

Warm Clothing

Thermals, woollen layers, windproof jacket - temperatures drop below 0°C at Kedarnath & Badrinath.

Valid ID Proof

Aadhaar, Voter ID or Passport is mandatory for all travellers. Keep multiple copies.

Trekking Shoes

Comfortable, grip-sole shoes are essential for Yamunotri and Kedarnath treks.

Basic Medicines

Altitude sickness pills, pain relievers, band-aids and any personal prescription medicines.

Day Backpack

A light 20-25L backpack for daily excursions with water bottle, snacks and essentials.

Rainwear / Poncho

Sudden rains are common in the Himalayas - a lightweight poncho or rain jacket is a must.

Pro Tips

Travel Tips

1

Start early each day. Temples get very crowded by 9 AM. Aim to reach by 6-7 AM for a peaceful darshan experience.

2

Stay hydrated. High altitude can cause dehydration quickly. Carry at least 2 litres of water daily and avoid alcohol.

3

Book Kedarnath helicopter in advance. Helicopter seats sell out weeks before - book as soon as you confirm your package.

4

Carry cash. ATMs are scarce beyond Uttarkashi and Guptkashi. Withdraw enough before leaving Haridwar.

5

Respect local customs. Remove footwear before temples, dress modestly, and maintain silence inside shrines.

Best Time

When to Visit

May - June

Peak season - pleasant weather, temples fully open, rhododendrons in bloom.

Sept - Oct

Post-monsoon - clear skies, best mountain views, less crowded temples.

July - Aug

Avoid - heavy monsoon, frequent landslides, road closures possible.

Nov - Apr

Temples closed - Char Dham portals shut for winter after Diwali typically.
